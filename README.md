# かんなぎ (Kannagi)

## Why I Make Software

The reason I started making software is simple. I could not find software that suited me.

Some software already meets 80% of my needs, but the remaining 20% is often what matters to me. It does not mean the software is bad. Everyone has different needs, habits, and preferences.

I like simple designs and software that requires little or no mental effort to use.

Simple does not mean fewer features. The technology behind the software can be complex, but using it does not have to be.

My projects often have more features than I introduce. Many of them are never announced or documented. I hope users can discover and use these features naturally through the interface itself.

I do not always try to create new technology. There are already many great technologies. Finding ways to combine them and make better use of them is also something I enjoy.

I care about performance, resource usage, ease of installation, and small details in daily use.

I like to achieve more with less.

## Development and Maintenance

All of these projects are developed in my free time outside my full-time job.

My time and resources are limited. I usually do not have a detailed testing plan or enough time to test all possible use cases, hardware, and system environments.

Bug reports, feedback, and suggestions are welcome. However, I cannot guarantee how quickly an issue will be fixed, when a feature will be added, or when a new version will be released.

## About Languages

My native language is Traditional Chinese. I also use Japanese and English.

You may notice that I use simple English, and sometimes my grammar or expressions may not sound very natural.

I mainly write in English because I want people from different countries to be able to read my documentation. Not everyone who reads it is a native English speaker.

I prefer common words, short sentences, and expressions that are easy to understand and translate.

Just like my software, I want my writing to require as little mental effort as possible.

Feel free to communicate with me in English, Traditional Chinese, or Japanese.

If they also happen to suit you, I am happy to share them with you.
